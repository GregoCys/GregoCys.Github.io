Endpoint Application Control & Browser Hardening

Engineering policy controls while preserving compatibility with business applications.

Intune / Group Policy / AppLocker / CIS ChromeProfessional workstream
This retrospective describes professional responsibilities at a high level. Organization-specific data, policy names, hostnames, identities, sensitive configuration details, and internal records are deliberately excluded. The description does not claim a completed enterprise-wide rollout unless documented.

Engineering problem

The work involved aligning browser configuration with CIS recommendations while addressing application-control failures and avoiding conflicting settings across Group Policy and Intune.

Methodology

  1. Compared Chrome security baseline requirements with the organization's existing Group Policy and Intune deployment approaches.
  2. Investigated AppLocker publisher, hash, and packaged-app rules using local enforcement events, signature information, and executable metadata.
  3. Designed pilot-based verification, backup and reporting steps, and rollback-aware changes for policies affecting business-critical applications.

Documented outcome

Documented policy decisions, verification commands, and application compatibility considerations. Pilot and configuration work are represented separately from any unverified full-environment deployment.

Identity & Session Security Engineering

Identity and endpoint policy engineering in a managed enterprise environment.

Microsoft Entra ID / Conditional Access / Windows 365Professional workstream
This retrospective describes professional responsibilities at a high level. Organization-specific data, policy names, hostnames, identities, sensitive configuration details, and internal records are deliberately excluded. The description does not claim a completed enterprise-wide rollout unless documented.

Engineering problem

The objective was to plan a controlled rollout of device-bound session credentials without disrupting essential authentication workflows. Policy rollout required consideration of TPM 2.0, supported clients, existing Windows 365 Cloud PCs, and emergency access accounts.

Methodology

  1. Reviewed device prerequisites and support constraints for token protection and device-bound credentials.
  2. Defined a report-only observation period followed by a scoped Helpdesk pilot, with exclusions for emergency-access accounts and Windows 365 Cloud PCs.
  3. Prepared Helpdesk communications, monitored expected authentication behavior, and documented rollout readiness and potential application impact.

Documented outcome

Produced a staged implementation approach with explicit pilot scope, exclusions, prerequisites, and validation criteria. Organization-wide enforcement or measured reduction in token theft is not claimed.

Identity & Session Security Engineering

Identity and endpoint policy engineering in a managed enterprise environment.

Microsoft Entra ID / Conditional Access / Windows 365Professional workstream
This case study describes the work and validation approach without disclosing internal policy names, tenant details, or user information.

Engineering problem

I worked on a staged rollout plan for device-bound session credentials. The challenge was to strengthen session protection while accounting for device readiness, browser support, Windows 365 Cloud PCs, and emergency access.

Planning and validation

  1. Checked device prerequisites, including Entra join state, TPM 2.0 readiness, and supported client behavior, before identifying a pilot population.
  2. Separated report-only observation from enforcement, defined a Helpdesk pilot, and excluded emergency-access accounts and Windows 365 Cloud PCs from the initial scope.
  3. Prepared Helpdesk communications and checks for authentication outcomes, client compatibility, and potential application impact.

Why the pilot mattered

Device eligibility and Conditional Access policy evaluation are different questions. A compatible browser or workstation still needs testing against the actual sign-in policy, exclusions, and user workflow. I treated report-only results as an input to pilot decisions, not proof that enforcement would have no impact.

Outcome

The deliverable was a phased rollout plan with defined prerequisites, exclusions, and pilot checks. The work documented here does not establish that organization-wide enforcement was completed.

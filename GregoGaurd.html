RESEARCH METHOD

How I approach a finding

01

Establish baseline

Identify the binary, verify hashes, and document expected operation.

02

Reproduce

Construct controlled inputs and capture behavior or failure evidence.

03

Analyze

Correlate debugging registers, memory, disassembly, and source-level logic.

04

Remediate & verify

Define a fix, retain originals, test boundaries, and document limitations.
